## ABOUT THE PROJECT

Wellora AI is an intelligent health and wellness platform designed to simplify personal health management through artificial intelligence and data-driven insights.

The platform brings health monitoring, personalized wellness suggestions, progress analytics, and AI-powered assistance together in one integrated application.

Its primary objective is to make health information easier to understand, monitor, and manage.

## CORE FEATURES

**01. AI HEALTH ASSISTANT**

An intelligent conversational assistant designed to provide health-related information and personalized wellness guidance.

**02. HEALTH MONITORING**

A centralized system for maintaining personal health information and monitoring health-related parameters.

**03. ANALYTICS & REPORTS**

Visual representation of health data, progress, and trends to support better understanding of personal wellness.

**04. PERSONALIZED DIET PLANNER**

A nutrition planning module designed around individual preferences and wellness goals.

**05. WORKOUT PLANNER**

A structured system for planning physical activities and tracking workout progress.

**06. SMART REMINDERS**

Reminders for daily wellness activities and healthy habits.
